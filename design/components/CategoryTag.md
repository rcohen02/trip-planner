# CategoryTag

A category tag names what kind of place something is: icon in the category color, name in mono `ink-2`.

- Colors: `cat-art`, `cat-food`, `cat-bar`, `cat-shop`, `cat-tour`, `cat-nature`, `cat-history`, `cat-festival`, `cat-walk`. The color goes on the icon, dot or pin, never on the text.
- Map pins (`tp-pin`): 14px discs with a 2px `surface` ring.
- Lisbon uses the first five. Nature, History and Festivals match the research priorities for family trips.
- Always pair the color with the icon or the word, because bar/art and festival/crit are close in hue.
