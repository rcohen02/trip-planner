# FlightCard

A flight card shows one leg: codes large, times and terminals under them, then the details people need at the airport.

- Airport codes in `figure`, times in the airport's local time with the zone when it isn't obvious.
- Details as a definition list: check-in closes, seats, booking ref and ticket numbers in `data` mono.
- Problems inline in `warn-ink` ("not together"); the matching task goes to Bookings & To-Do.
