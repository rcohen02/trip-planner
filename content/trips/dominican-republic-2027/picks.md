# Dominican Republic (Feb 13–20, 2027)

Stay: Casablanca Boutique Hotel, Cabrera. Nature first; family-friendly; nothing on hotels. Everything here is within 90 minutes of the hotel.

## Nature

- **Playa Grande** — Río San Juan area, about 10–20 min from the hotel (est.). Quiet, undeveloped beach with bigger waves, good for windsurfing. Free. [Info](https://justinpluslauren.com/best-beaches-in-dominican-republic/) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+Grande+Dominican+Republic)
  *2026:* Cabrera Chalet is nearby.

- **Playa Caleton** — Near Río San Juan, about 20–30 min from the hotel (est.). Small crescent beach with a local feel and few facilities. Free. [Info](https://justinpluslauren.com/best-beaches-in-dominican-republic/) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+Caleton+Dominican+Republic)
  *2026:* Few amenities.

- **Playa Cabarete (Kite Beach)** — Cabarete, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Kiteboarding and windsurfing beach with beginner schools. Free. [Info](https://justinpluslauren.com/best-beaches-in-dominican-republic/) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Kite+Beach+Cabarete)
  *2026:* Sunbathing and beginner lessons.

- **Playa Sosúa** — Sosúa, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Golden sand with a coral reef for snorkeling. Free. [Info](https://justinpluslauren.com/best-beaches-in-dominican-republic/) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+Sosua+Dominican+Republic)
  *2026:* Good for snorkeling.

- **Playa Encuentro** — Cabarete area, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Surf beach with surf schools, not for calm swimming. Free. [Info](https://justinpluslauren.com/best-beaches-in-dominican-republic/) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Playa+Encuentro+Dominican+Republic)
  *2026:* Right-hand reef break.

- **Blue Lagoon** — Cabrera area, a few minutes from Laguna Dudú. Two lakes connected by underground tunnels, with a zip line from the highest point. Price not found. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Blue+Lagoon/@19.5568583,-70.0562586,12z/data=!4m9!1m2!2m1!1scenote!3m5!1s0x8eae69d30279b22d:0x6609e9713921a481!8m2!3d19.5568583!4d-69.9100329!16s%2Fg%2F1hm38kf0z?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAyMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
  *2026:* Hours not found. Check on arrival.

- **Balneario El Saltadero** — Cabrera, about 5–10 min from the hotel (est.). Natural waterfall with a large swimming pool, surrounded by rocky cliffs and vegetation. Stairs lead down to the lake, and locals jump in. Trails nearby lead to small ponds and caves. Price not found. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Balneario+El+Saltadero/@19.6417741,-69.993574,12z/data=!4m16!1m8!2m7!1sThings+to+do!3m5!2sBlue+Lagoon!3s0x8eae69d30279b22d:0x6609e9713921a481!4m2!1d-69.9100329!2d19.5568583!3m6!1s0x8eae69ae9de0b6b1:0x68ba8a398b3895a2!8m2!3d19.6410272!4d-69.9171019!15sCgxUaGluZ3MgdG8gZG9aDiIMdGhpbmdzIHRvIGRvkgESdG91cmlzdF9hdHRyYWN0aW9umgFEQ2k5RFFVbFJRVU52WkVOb2RIbGpSamx2VDJ4YU5rNHhPRFZVTTFVeldsYzVNRk50YTNSVmJrSkVWRU14VTFaWFl4QULgAQD6AQQIQhAw!16s%2Fg%2F11gp3sc58v?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAyMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
  *2026:* Hours not found. No entrance fee listed in sources.

- **Cuevas de Cabarete** — 1 km west of Cabarete, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Guided tour of six caves, about 120 min, with an optional dip in an underwater lagoon. Tours in English, Spanish and German. RD$1,530–2,140 per tour (≈ US$25–35). [Site](https://showcaves.com/english/do/showcaves/Cabarete.html) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cuevas+de+Cabarete)
  *2026:* Hours not listed. The site's "195 Pts" adult price is unclear.

- **Parque Nacional El Choco** — About 15 min from Cabarete, about 1 h 15 min from the hotel (est.). Hiking, biking, swimming, boating and birdwatching, with bilingual guides. Pairs well with the caves. RD$1,530–2,140 per tour (≈ US$25–35). [Info](https://trip101.com/article/things-to-do-in-cabarete-dominican-republic) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=El+Choco+National+Park+Cabarete)
  *2026:* Hours not found.

## Tours & Getting Around

- **Laguna Gri-Gri** — Río San Juan, about 30 min from the hotel (est.). Boat tour through a mangrove lagoon and bird sanctuary. The tour stops at Cueva de Las Golondrinas for a swim in a shallow turquoise cove and ends at Playa Caleton. Daily guided tours. Price not found. [Info](https://www.godominicanrepublic.com/things-to-do/laguna-gri-gri) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Laguna+Gri-Gri/@19.6464676,-70.2650089,10z/data=!4m6!3m5!1s0x8eae1497084f047b:0xe19a634b506ca344!8m2!3d19.6467714!4d-70.0775141!16s%2Fg%2F11c528qjwb?entry=ttu&g_ep=EgoyMDI2MTAyMC4wIKXMDSoASAFQAw%3D%3D)
  *2026:* Best at sunset for egrets and vultures over the mangroves. Prices and hours not found.

- **Monkey Jungle & Zip Line Adventures** — Cabarete, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Zip lining, squirrel monkey encounters and a seven-station gun range. Guide required. A full-day package with transport and a bus tour is also offered. RD$1,224–3,365 per person (≈ US$20–55). [Info](https://trip101.com/article/things-to-do-in-cabarete-dominican-republic) [Info](https://www.musement.com/uk/puerto-plata/monkey-jungle-tour-178259/) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Monkey+Jungle+Zip+Line+Cabarete)
  *2026:* Listed daily 9 am–4 pm. Full-day package 4–8 h from about £68 per person (≈ US$90, est.). Not confirmed on the venue's site.

- **2-Person Dune Buggy Tour** — Cabarete, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Countryside tour, 4.5 h, with gear and instructors included. ~RD$8,304 per 2-person buggy (≈ US$135.71). [Info](https://trip101.com/article/things-to-do-in-cabarete-dominican-republic) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cabarete+dune+buggy)
  *2026:* Start times not listed.

- **Four-Wheeler Tour** — Cabarete, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). About 4 h, an alternative to the buggy. ~RD$7,016 (≈ US$114.67). [Info](https://trip101.com/article/things-to-do-in-cabarete-dominican-republic) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Cabarete+four+wheeler+tour)
  *2026:* Start times not listed.

- **Yasica River Kayak Tour** — Cabarete and Sosúa area, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). About 5 h on the river with lunch and a snack. Hotel pickup and drop-off included. Minimum age 5. Book ahead. From ~RD$2,695 (≈ US$44.05). [Info](https://www.civitatis.com/en/cabarete/yasica-river-kayak-tour) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Yasica+River+Cabarete)
  *2026:* Bookings open up to 2 days ahead when seats are free. Adult and child prices not listed.

- **Private Kayak or Paddleboard Tour, La Boca** — La Boca, Cabarete, about 1 hr from the hotel (est.). Private 2-hour tour, maximum 8 people. Kayak or paddleboard. ~RD$3,977 per person (≈ US$65). [Info](https://www.expedia.com.my/things-to-do/2-hour-private-kayak-or-paddleboard-tour-in-cabarete-la-boca.a46422224.activity-details) [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=La+Boca+Cabarete+kayak)

## Shopping

- **Nagua Market** — Nagua town center, about 40 min from the hotel (est.). Produce and food market with fruit, plantains and cheese, plus mango, passion fruit and cacao from the hills. Cheap breakfast and lunch, with nobody selling to tourists. RD$120–250 per meal (≈ US$2–4). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Nagua+Market)
  *2026:* Hours not listed. Go before noon; the market gets hot by late morning. Allow about 45 minutes.
