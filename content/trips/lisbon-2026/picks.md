## Art & Museums

- **MAAT (Museum of Art, Architecture and Technology)** — Belém/Junqueira riverfront. €16 (≈ $19). Hours not confirmed; usually closed Tuesdays. [Site](https://www.maat.pt/en/plan-a-visit)
  *2026:* Futuristic seashell-like gallery joined to a restored 1908 power station; big Pop Art collection alongside old turbines and machinery.
- **MACAM (Museu de Arte Contemporânea Armando Martins)** — Rua da Junqueira 66. €15 + €1.20 fee (≈ $19). Hours not confirmed. [Site](https://macam.pt/en)
  *2026:* ~1-year-old museum in an 18th-century palace; 600+ works from a developer's collection — Portuguese art late 1800s–1980s plus newer work.
- **Museu do Tesouro Real (Crown Jewels) + Palácio Nacional da Ajuda** — Calçada da Ajuda. ✓ Daily 10:00–18:00 from Oct (last entry 17:00). Treasury €11 (≈ $13); article's combined price €13 (≈ $15) is from 2023. [Site](https://www.tesouroreal.pt/en)
  *2023:* Royal diamonds, emeralds and a gold-and-sapphire bookmark in a sleek new museum; next-door palace has a white-bar façade, chandeliers, tapestries and an El Greco.
- **Casa Fernando Pessoa** — Rua Coelho da Rocha 16–18, Campo de Ourique. ✓ Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00, closed Mon. €5 (≈ $6). [Site](https://www.casafernandopessoa.pt/en/visit)
  *2026:* The poet's home; bedroom kept as he left it, plus a case of his oval rimless glasses.
- **Casa-Museu Amália Rodrigues** — Rua de São Bento. Hours/price not confirmed. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Casa-Museu+Amalia+Rodrigues+Lisboa)
  *2023:* The fado diva's house on the São Bento walk. Also hosts "Fado in the Garden" through Oct 31.
- **Galeria Filomena Soares** — Beato. Free. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Galeria+Filomena+Soares+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Beato institution since 2001 (Shirin Neshat, Allan Sekula). Go up to the roof for Dan Graham's curving glass-wall installation.
- **Galeria Francisco Fino** — Marvila. Free. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Galeria+Francisco+Fino+Lisboa)
  *2023:* White two-level house showing mostly Portuguese and Brazilian contemporary artists.

> Commercial galleries often close Sun–Mon — aim for Fri or Sat.

## Restaurants

- **Canalha** — Rua da Junqueira 207, Belém. ~€40 (≈ $46)/person; Michelin Bib Gourmand. Call +351 962 152 742. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Canalha+Rua+da+Junqueira+207+Lisboa)
  *2026:* Lisbon chefs' favorite, tough reservation. Shrimp-and-onion omelet €18 (≈ $21), squid in ewe's-milk butter €25 (≈ $29), steak €16 (≈ $19). **Book now.**
- **Santa Joana (Nuno Mendes)** — In Locke de Santa Joana, near Marquês de Pombal. Pork T-bone with walnut sauce €32 (≈ $37). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Santa+Joana+restaurant+Lisboa)
  *2026:* "Superb dinner" from a renowned chef; the standout tiborna is currently off the menu.
- **Pigmeu** — Campo de Ourique. Lunch ~€17 (≈ $20). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pigmeu+Campo+de+Ourique)
  *2026:* Excellent free-range Portuguese pork. Pairs with nearby Casa Pessoa.
- **Damas** — Graça. ~€20 (≈ $23) with wine. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Damas+Graca+Lisboa)
  *2026:* "Wonderfully funky" restaurant/bar/art space in an old industrial bakery. Get the pica pau and string-bean tempura.
- **Arkhe** — Near Cais do Sodré. 5-course vegetarian tasting €70 (≈ $81) without drinks. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Arkhe+restaurante+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Quietly elegant, plant-filled room; artful dishes like beet several ways in dashi, kimchi tortellini.
- **Tricky's** — Near Cais do Sodré. ~€70 (≈ $81) for two without drinks. Cash only (2023). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Tricky%27s+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Industrial décor, natural wines, eclectic small plates (mackerel wrapped in lardo).
- **Pica-Pau** — Príncipe Real. ~€50 (≈ $58) for two. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Pica-Pau+Principe+Real+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Old-time farmhouse and fisherman's food; cod cakes €6 (≈ $7), monkfish-and-shrimp rice; nice back patio.

## Bars & Nightlife

- **BacoAlto** — Bairro Alto. Chorizo €9.50 (≈ $11). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=BacoAlto+Lisboa)
  *2026:* Wine bar with wooden stools; ask for a red from the little-known Bairrada region.
- **Side Bar** — Rua da Barroca, Bairro Alto. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Side+Bar+Rua+da+Barroca+Lisboa)
  *2026:* Friendly, very mixed crowd in the Bairro Alto gay-bar cluster; good G&Ts.
- **Monkey Mash / Red Frog** — Off Avenida da Liberdade. Cocktails €13.50–15 (≈ $16–17). Book ahead. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Monkey+Mash+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Tropical-chic underground cocktail bar with a hidden speakeasy (Red Frog) behind a wall panel.
- **Gala Cricri at Palácio do Grilo** — Beato. Free if reserved online or before 22:00, else €8 (≈ $9); cocktails €10–14 (≈ $12–16). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Palacio+do+Grilo+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Late-night dance party through the rooms, gardens and chapel of an 18th-century palace; electro to disco.
- **Little Chelsea Experience** — Marvila. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Little+Chelsea+Experience+Lisboa)
  *2023:* 1970s NY punk-and-glam boutique/bar/gallery — vintage clothes, wine, rock shows.
- **Dois Corvos** — Marvila. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Dois+Corvos+Marvila)
  *2023:* Art-decorated craft-beer hall on the same block.
- **Maria Limão at Miradouro da Senhora do Monte** — Graça. Caipirinha €7.50 (≈ $9). [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Miradouro+da+Senhora+do+Monte)
  *2023:* Drinks stand at the city's best sunset viewpoint — rooftops, castle and the Tagus.

## Shopping

- **Feira da Ladra flea market** — Campo de Santa Clara, behind São Vicente de Fora. ✓ Tuesdays & Saturdays (Sat Oct 10 works). Free. [Info](https://www.visitlisboa.com/en/places/feira-da-ladra-flea-market)
  *2023:* Bric-a-brac that improves uphill (glassware, china, jewelry). Look for the yellow van of art-house DVDs and Frida/Dalí hand-painted military jackets.
- **Rua de São Bento strip** — São Bento. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rua+de+Sao+Bento+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Antique shops mixed with new creative stores:
  - **Homecore** — French-owned, mostly single-color fashion
  - **Sokyo Lisbon** — melted/meteorite-like Japanese ceramics
  - **Distopia** — bookshop with "Soul of Lisbon" and "Secret Lisbon" guides, plus vinyl
- **Feed** — Beato. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Feed+Beato+Lisboa)
  *2023:* Hangar-size store of funky, psychedelic women's wear and accessories.

## Tours & Getting Around

- **Queer Lisbon walking tour (guide Leonor Machado)** — Starts at Jardim do Príncipe Real. €40 (≈ $46), 4 hours. [Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Jardim+do+Principe+Real)
  *2026:* Changed how the writer sees the city — ties fascism and colonial history to Lisbon today. Search "Queer Lisbon tour Leonor Machado" to book.
- **Tram 12E** — From Martim Moniz. €3 (≈ $3.50), 2023 price.
  *2023:* Skip the Tram 28 lines — same vintage cars, ~30 min through Mouraria, Portas do Sol, Alfama and the cathedral. Note: funiculars closed after the Sept 2025 crash; check if reopened.
