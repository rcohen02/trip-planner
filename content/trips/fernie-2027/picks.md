# Fernie & the Rockies (July 3–10, 2027)

Stays: Banff (July 3–5), our house in Fernie (July 5–8), Glacier area (July 8–10). Nature first; family-friendly; nothing on hotels.

## Tours & getting around

- **Banff Legacy Trail** — Bow Valley Parkway (start at Cascade Ponds) to the Banff East Gate, 22.3 km of paved path. Shared with walkers and skaters; rolling, with a few short steep marked hills. Pedestrians use the pedestrian gate at the electrified mats. [Site](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/cyclisme-biking/cyclismeroute-roadcycling/heritage-legacy)
- **Tunnel49 Adventures** — Fernie, on the Elk River. Full-day whitewater (Class 2–4, 16 km through Elk Canyon) and a half-day Ghostrider family trip for ages 3+. Phone 250-423-5008. [Site](https://t49.ca/adventures/)
- **Glacier Raft Company** — West Glacier, on the Middle Fork of the Flathead River. Half-day whitewater, 2–3.5 hours; from $50 USD per adult or child (12 and under) plus 8% service fee and guide tip. Minimum age varies by month (about 5–12) and 50 lb. Booking 1-406-888-5454. [Site](https://www.glacierparkcollection.com/activities/glacier-raft-co/half-day-trips/half-day-whitewater/)

- **Bear Street Outfitters (bike rental)** — 203a Bear St, Banff. Standard and e-bikes, well reviewed for trail and Bow Valley rides. +1 877-447-1934. [Site](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Bear+Street+Outfitters+Banff)
- **Black Tie Ski & Bike Rentals (bike rental)** — 201 Lynx St, Banff, at Banff Park Lodge. E-bikes; reviewers say book early in summer. +1 825-398-9563. [Site](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Black+Tie+Ski+Bike+Rentals+Banff)
- **GearHub Sports (bike rental)** — 401 1st Ave, Fernie. Bikes and e-bikes, with trail advice from staff. +1 250-423-5555. [Site](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=GearHub+Sports+Fernie)
- **Canyon Raft Company** — Elk River, Elko BC, about 30 min from Fernie. Guided Elk River float with cliff jumping; about 4 hours on the river, photos about $10 per person. +1 250-423-7226. [Site](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Canyon+Raft+Company+Elko+BC)
- **Rocky Mountain Raft Tours** — Bow River, Banff. Relaxed scenic float, about 45 minutes on the water, not whitewater. Good for families. +1 403-762-3632. [Site](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Rocky+Mountain+Raft+Tours+Banff)
- **Chinook Rafting** — Kananaskis River, about 1.5 hours from Banff. Grade 2–3 rapids, family-friendly, free photos from some guides. +1 866-330-7238. [Site](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Chinook+Rafting+Riverbase+Kananaskis)

## Nature

- **Surveyors Lake** — Kikomun Creek Provincial Park, about 30 min south of Fernie. Sandy beaches, shade, and a day-use area beside the campground. Described as warm and good for families. [Info](https://tourismfernie.com/activities/swimming)
- **Tie Lake** — BC Forest Recreation Site, about 45 min south of Fernie near Jaffray. Good for swimming, boating and camping. Facilities and water temperature not confirmed. [Info](https://tourismfernie.com/activities/swimming)
- **Fairy Creek Falls Trail** — Fernie, about 4 km round trip, gentle. Cascading, misty waterfall, best in early summer. Starts at the Fernie Visitor Information Centre on Hwy 3. [Info](https://tourismfernie.com/blog/get-the-whole-family-out-on-these-easy-fernie-hikes)
- **Peyto Lake Viewpoint** — Banff area, 2.6 km round trip, 80 m gain. Views of Peyto Lake from its second viewpoint. [Info](https://10adventures.com/10-best-family-hikes-in-banff-national-park/)
- **Moraine Lake Rockpile** — Banff area, 3.4 km, 38 m gain. Moraine Lake and the Rockpile lookout. [Info](https://10adventures.com/10-best-family-hikes-in-banff-national-park/)
- **Bow Lake Lakeshore** — Banff area, 3.4 km, flat. Lakeshore walk with Crowfoot Mountain views. [Info](https://10adventures.com/10-best-family-hikes-in-banff-national-park/)
- **Johnston Canyon** — Banff area, 6.3 km, 122 m gain. Lower and Upper Falls, with an optional Ink Pots side loop. [Info](https://10adventures.com/10-best-family-hikes-in-banff-national-park/)
- **Johnson Lake** — Banff area, 3 km lakeside loop, flat, open year-round. [Info](https://10adventures.com/10-best-family-hikes-in-banff-national-park/)
- **Consolation Lakes** — Banff area, 5.8 km, 135 m gain. Lakes and the Tower of Babel. [Info](https://10adventures.com/10-best-family-hikes-in-banff-national-park/)
- **Fernie Community Trail** — City multi-use trail along the Elk River in Fernie, also groomed in winter. Surface not confirmed as paved. [Info](https://www.fernie.ca/EN/main/residents/parks-recreation/outdoor-activities.html)
- **Coal Creek Heritage Trail** — Mountain bike route to the old townsite near Fernie. Not paved; for stronger riders. [Info](https://www.fernie.ca/EN/main/residents/parks-recreation/outdoor-activities.html)
