# Fernie & the Rockies (July 3–10, 2027)

Stays: Banff (July 3–5), our house in Fernie (July 5–8), Glacier area (July 8–10). Nature first; family-friendly; nothing on hotels.

## Tours & getting around

- **Banff Legacy Trail** — Bow Valley Parkway (start at Cascade Ponds) to the Banff East Gate, 22.3 km of paved path. Shared with walkers and skaters; rolling, with a few short steep marked hills. Pedestrians use the pedestrian gate at the electrified mats. [Site](https://parks.canada.ca/pn-np/ab/banff/activ/cyclisme-biking/cyclismeroute-roadcycling/heritage-legacy)
- **Tunnel49 Adventures** — Fernie, on the Elk River. Full-day whitewater (Class 2–4, 16 km through Elk Canyon) and a half-day Ghostrider family trip for ages 3+. Phone 250-423-5008. [Site](https://t49.ca/adventures/)
- **Glacier Raft Company** — West Glacier, on the Middle Fork of the Flathead River. Half-day whitewater, 2–3.5 hours; from $50 USD per adult or child (12 and under) plus 8% service fee and guide tip. Minimum age varies by month (about 5–12) and 50 lb. Booking 1-406-888-5454. [Site](https://www.glacierparkcollection.com/activities/glacier-raft-co/half-day-trips/half-day-whitewater/)

## Nature

- **Fernie Community Trail** — City multi-use trail along the Elk River in Fernie, also groomed in winter. Surface not confirmed as paved. [Info](https://www.fernie.ca/EN/main/residents/parks-recreation/outdoor-activities.html)
- **Coal Creek Heritage Trail** — Mountain bike route to the old townsite near Fernie. Not paved; for stronger riders. [Info](https://www.fernie.ca/EN/main/residents/parks-recreation/outdoor-activities.html)
